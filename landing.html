Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from Google Calendar?

Google Calendar is for appointments. PrepMaster is for outcomes. We track syllabus completion percentages, offer subject-specific templates, and provide analytics on your study efficiency, which a standard calendar cannot do.

What exams are supported?

Currently optimized for UPSC CSE, State PCS (UP/Bihar/MP), SSC CGL, and RRB. However, the custom planner works for any syllabus-based exam.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your study patterns and data are private. We do not sell user data to coaching institutes.
